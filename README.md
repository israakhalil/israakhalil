## 🚀 About Me

I'm a data scientist and software engineer from Gaza with over ten years of experience building data-driven systems for government, travel and education. I build machine learning models, and I also build the databases, APIs and applications that put those models and their data to work.

- 📄 Published research on **Arabic fake-news detection** with deep learning and Transformers: AraBERTv2 reached **97% accuracy** (*Expert Systems with Applications*, 2024)
- ✈️ Worked remotely for 5.5 years with a travel company in Kuwait, integrating flight (Travelport, SOAP/XML) and hotel supplier APIs into an in-house booking platform that **cut software costs by about 70%**
- 🏛️ Built Laravel and Vue applications on **Oracle** for payroll, budget and reporting at the Palestinian Ministry of Finance
- 🧠 Interested in **Machine Learning**, **NLP** (especially Arabic text) and **data pipelines**
- 🌱 Currently learning **Docker**, **CI/CD** and **AI-assisted development** with the Claude API and Claude Code

## 📂 Featured Projects

### ⭐ [Arabic Fake News Detection](https://github.com/motazsaad/Arabic-Fakenews) *(main contributor)* · [Dataset](https://github.com/israakhalil/ArabicFake_News)

> Master's research on detecting Arabic misinformation. I wrote scrapers for three Arabic fact-checking platforms (Akeed, Fatabyyano, Misbar), collected about 4,000 labelled claims, and built a curated dataset of 2,000 articles. I then compared BiLSTM and BiGRU with Arabic Transformers (AraBERT, AraELECTRA, QARiB, ARBERT/MARBERT). **AraBERTv2 reached 97% accuracy and 0.97 F1.** Published in *Expert Systems with Applications* (2024).
