## 🚀 About Me

I live at the intersection of **data** and **the web**. On one side, I explore datasets, build machine learning models, and uncover the stories hidden in numbers. On the other, I design reliable databases and craft clean, secure **RESTful APIs** with **Laravel** that bring those insights to life.

- 🔭 Working on **data-driven web applications** that combine analytics with solid backend engineering
- 🧠 Passionate about **Machine Learning**, **NLP** (especially Arabic text), and **Data Analysis**
- ⚙️ Building scalable backends with **Laravel**, **MySQL**, and well-documented **APIs**
- 🌱 Always learning — currently deepening my skills in **Deep Learning** and **data pipelines**
- 💬 Ask me about **Python, Laravel, SQL, APIs, and data visualization**
